The person behind the systems

I learned engineering
in the control room.

My first engineering job was automating mainframe operations for banking systems in Italy. A missed step wasn’t just an inconvenience. It could become a production incident.

That taught me a habit I’ve carried through every job since: don’t just fix the problem. Understand why it happened, then build something that makes it less likely to happen again.

I took that mindset to AWS, from critical incident response to technical account management. Today at GitLab, I help engineering organisations make AI and DevSecOps part of how they actually work.
